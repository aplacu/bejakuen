# Bejakeun product roadmap

## Positioning
Bejakeun is the brand for an AI sales workforce platform. The first product is Bejakeun Sales Agent. Property is one possible vertical, not the company identity.

## Phase 0: Reposition the brand
- [x] Reframe the product from lead-tracking/pilot service to AI sales workforce.
- [x] Redesign landing page, product narrative, and visual system.
- [x] Add interactive concept views for sales agent, pipeline, and revenue insights.
- [x] Clarify that the demo is fictional and not connected to live systems.
- [ ] Run automated test, build, and lint checks on the rebuilt experience.

## Phase 1: Validate the wedge
- [ ] Interview 20 businesses across no more than two initial verticals.
- [ ] Compare lead volume, response delays, follow-up gaps, sales cycle, and willingness to pay.
- [ ] Select one initial workflow and define a measurable baseline.
- [ ] Secure at least one paid pilot before expanding scope.

## Phase 2: Production MVP
- [ ] Lead inbox and normalized conversation model.
- [ ] Approved business knowledge with source traceability.
- [ ] Lead qualification and editable pipeline stages.
- [ ] Follow-up tasks with consent and opt-out handling.
- [ ] Human handoff, audit log, and clear permission boundaries.
- [ ] Usage and cost monitoring per workspace.

## Phase 3: Repeatable SaaS
- [ ] Tenant isolation, roles, onboarding, and workspace settings.
- [ ] Official channel integrations with provider-policy compliance.
- [ ] Evaluation suite for accuracy, escalation, and action safety.
- [ ] Billing, usage limits, retention metrics, and support playbooks.
- [ ] Standardized industry workflows and partner distribution.

## Product guardrails
- No claims of live AI capability until it exists and is tested.
- No fabricated sales uplift, testimonials, customer logos, or operational metrics.
- No autonomous price changes, binding promises, or sensitive actions without authorization.
- Customer data must be isolated, access-controlled, and used only under agreed permissions.
